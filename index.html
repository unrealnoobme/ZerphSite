One build, one target.

Zerph is compiled for Minecraft 1.21.11 on Fabric — nothing else, so nothing runs against a mismatched game jar.

The build

Ships as a Fabric mod jar. Drop it into your mods folder alongside Fabric Loader — Zerph loads with the rest of your mods, no separate injector.

Before you install

  • Java17 or newer
  • RAM allocated4 GB minimum
  • LauncherVanilla, MultiMC, or Prism
  • LoaderFabric
  • Server-side rulescheck before joining

Some servers disallow client modifications. Zerph won't stop you from joining one that does — that part's on you.

About Zerph

Built to feel like a real client.

Zerph combines a compact ClickGUI-inspired design with configurable modules, profiles and client settings. The interface stays out of your way until you need it, with subtle motion, responsive controls and a focused dark theme.

Zerph Client — an independent project, not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
